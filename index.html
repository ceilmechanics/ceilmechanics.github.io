Wendan Jiang (she/her)

PhD Student in Computer Science + STEM Education
Tufts University

I am a first-year PhD student in Computer Science and STEM Education at Tufts University, advised by Prof. Fahad Dogar and Prof. Milo Koretsky. My research lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and STEM Education Research. I draw on learning theories and human-centered design methods to design, develop, and evaluate equitable, engaging, and effective AI-powered learning technologies in real-world educational setting, particularly undergrad CS classrooms.

Before my PhD, I earned a B.S. in Computer Science from the University of Michigan, and worked two years as a software engineer in industry.
